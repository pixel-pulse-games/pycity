PyCity is a top-down city builder for Windows. Place roads, farms, factories and houses, then watch trucks carry food and goods across the map you built.

Download for Windows Free. Current version: v1.1.0.
Click grass to lay a road. Click a road to remove it. Deliveries: 0

How the city works

Every farm, factory and house belongs to one of two chains, grain or timber, and only trades with buildings in its own chain. A building needs a road beside it to take part.

  1. Farms grow food

    When enough has grown, a truck carries it to a factory that needs it.

  2. Factories make goods

    They turn the food into goods and send a truck to a house that wants them.

  3. Houses pay tax

    They ask for goods as they fill up with demand. Keep that demand met and the city runs smoothly.

Busy road tiles slow trucks down, so a map with one road for everything jams up. Houses far from a police station slowly attract theft, and a station protects about nine tiles around it.

What’s new

Game speedv1.1.0
Press Tab to run the city at 1x, 2x or 4x. Growth, tax, crime and trucks all speed up together.
Modsv1.0.1
PyCity can now load Lua scripts from a mods folder to change costs, tax, crime and traffic. Mods run in a sandbox with no access to your files. Read the modding guide.
